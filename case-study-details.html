HR Foundation & Performance Restructure Demo

An illustrative scenario detailing the transformation of informal people practices into a scalable HR operating model.

Demonstration Notice: This case study template illustrates the analytical structure, diagnostic criteria, and implementation roadmaps utilized in our consulting practice. All company contexts and figures are conceptual demo data.

Industry Focus: Enterprise Technology Services
Organization Scale: 120 FTE Personnel (Fast Growing)
Engagement Domain: HR Foundation & Performance Management
Engagement Duration: 14 Weeks (Illustrative Schedule)

1. Organizational Context & Initial State

The company had expanded rapidly from a seed-stage team of 25 individuals to over 120 employees across engineering, sales, and client delivery functions. While technical output remained robust, operational leadership identified emerging symptoms of workforce friction: uncoordinated hiring practices, lack of written job bands, absence of formal performance appraisals, and informal leave tracking.

2. Core Consulting Objectives

  • Establish clear job grading architecture and role profiles across all departments.
  • Design an objective, transparent quarterly performance evaluation framework.
  • Compile a modern, compliant Employee Handbook standardizing workplace policies.
  • Enable line managers with structured evaluation rubrics and feedback guidelines.

3. Assessment Approach & Diagnostic Observations

During the initial four-week assessment phase, our advisory team conducted 18 structured stakeholder interviews and reviewed existing personnel documentation. The diagnostic revealed:

Diagnostic Finding A

74% of employees lacked written role profiles, leading to recurring task ambiguity between middle managers.

Diagnostic Finding B

Performance reviews were conducted once annually with open-ended text fields, producing subjective manager ratings.

4. Recommended HR Framework & Implementation Roadmap

Our consulting team engineered a four-tiered job architecture establishing standard titles, competency milestones, and salary bands. Additionally, we formulated an OKR-aligned quarterly review cycle structured around objective performance criteria.

Timeline Milestone Stage Key Implementation Deliverable
Weeks 1 – 3 Discovery & Audit Baseline HR Audit Report & Role Clarification Workshops
Weeks 4 – 7 System Design Standard Job Architecture Manual & Draft Employee Handbook
Weeks 8 – 11 Manager Enablement Manager Coaching Workshops & Objective Performance Rubrics
Weeks 12 – 14 Go-Live & Support First Review Cycle Launch & 90-Day Implementation Review

5. Expected Process Considerations

Successful execution requires active leadership sponsorship, open internal communication regarding why job bands are introduced, and ongoing support for first-time people managers as they conduct structured quarterly reviews.
