A Practical Approach to Building Stronger HR Systems

A structured, evidence-based five-stage consulting methodology that ensures sustainable people practices.

Disciplined Advisory

How We Deliver Measurable Workforce Improvement

Successful HR transformations do not rely on generic templates or isolated fixes. They require an objective assessment of systemic operational causes followed by structured execution.

Our five-stage methodology provides a predictable, collaborative framework that aligns executive leadership, operational managers, and people practices from inception to long-term adoption.

Framework Overview

The 5 Stages of Our HR Consulting Process

Each stage delivers transparent milestones, documented artifacts, and actionable managerial clarity.

Stage 1 Initial Discovery Phase

Understand: Strategic Alignment & Discovery

We interview key founders, managing directors, and department leaders to understand overall commercial priorities, historical organizational challenges, and future headcount objectives.

Core Objective:

Map business targets directly against current workforce capabilities.

Key Questions:

What are the growth bottlenecks? Where are manager friction points?

Deliverables:

Engagement Charter, Stakeholder Interview Synthesis, Scope Document.

Stage 2 Operational Assessment

Assess: Process & Documentation Review

We inspect employee lifecycle documentation, employment agreements, leave records, appraisal forms, and hiring workflows against industry standards.

Core Objective:

Evaluate process rigor, data consistency, and documentation hygiene.

Key Questions:

Are existing policies applied consistently? Are employee records centralized?

Deliverables:

HR Baseline Audit Scorecard, Document Inventory, Risk Heatmap.

Stage 3 Root-Cause Analysis

Diagnose: Gap Identification & Prioritization

Our advisors synthesize assessment observations into clear systemic findings, categorizing items by organizational impact and implementation complexity.

Core Objective:

Isolate systemic weaknesses rather than treating superficial symptoms.

Key Questions:

Why is turnover concentrated in specific teams? Why do reviews stall?

Deliverables:

Comprehensive Diagnostic Report, Priority Matrix, Executive Briefing.

Stage 4 System Architecture

Design: Framework & Policy Formulation

We author customized HR policies, job grading bands, recruitment assessment rubrics, employee handbooks, and performance review instruments.

Core Objective:

Build custom, scalable, and intuitive people systems for daily operations.

Key Questions:

Are policies easy for line managers to administer? Are metrics transparent?

Deliverables:

Employee Handbook, Job Banding Manual, Appraisal Forms, SOP Guides.

Stage 5 Execution Enablement

Support: Implementation & Manager Enablement

We facilitate managerial training workshops, guide rollout communications, and conduct 30/60/90-day progress checks to ensure adoption.

Core Objective:

Embed new behaviors and procedures into daily operational habits.

Key Questions:

Are teams completing reviews on schedule? Are candidates scored via rubrics?

Deliverables:

Manager Training Deck, Rollout Timeline, Post-Implementation Audit.
