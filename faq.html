Answers to common questions about HR consulting, workforce planning, recruitment advisory, and engagement scopes.

Everything You Need to Know About Our Advisory

Browse answers to frequent inquiries across our functional human capital practice domains.

What does an HR consulting company do?

An HR consulting company helps organizations structure, optimize, and manage their workforce practices. This includes drafting policies, building recruitment pipelines, designing performance review frameworks, conducting compliance audits, and aligning people strategies with business growth.

How can HR consulting help a growing business?

As companies grow beyond early team sizes, informal people management breaks down. HR consulting helps establish scalable systems, reduces costly hiring mistakes, resolves manager inconsistencies, improves employee retention, and ensures documentation hygiene.

Do you help startups establish HR systems from scratch?

Yes. We frequently consult with early-stage and seed-funded companies to create foundational HR infrastructure: standard offer letter templates, an employee handbook, initial job bands, and structured hiring checklists.

Can you develop a customized employee handbook for our company?

Yes. We develop comprehensive, tailored employee handbooks reflecting your company's values, culture, and operational realities, including attendance, leave, remote working, conduct, and grievance procedures.

Do you provide recruitment consulting?

Yes. Our recruitment consulting optimizes internal hiring processes. We author competency-based job descriptions, design interview scoring rubrics, train hiring managers, and streamline recruiter tracking metrics.

Can you help with workforce and headcount planning?

Yes. We evaluate current employee utilization, conduct skills gap analyses, model multi-scenario headcount projections, and align recruitment budgets with projected business revenue.

Do you support employee onboarding processes?

Yes. We design structured 30-60-90 day onboarding frameworks, orientation schedules, and manager checklists that accelerate new hire time-to-productivity and reduce early turnover.

Can you design performance management systems?

Yes. We replace subjective annual appraisals with objective quarterly OKR/KPI frameworks, continuous 1-on-1 check-in guides, 360-degree feedback tools, and structured performance improvement plans (PIPs).

What is included in an operational HR audit?

An operational HR audit systematically inspects personnel files, onboarding records, leave tracking systems, separation documentation, and managerial review cadences to identify inefficiencies and administrative gaps.

Can you help measure and improve employee engagement?

Yes. We formulate anonymous pulse surveys, conduct culture assessments, analyze feedback themes, and equip department leaders with actionable retention and recognition blueprints.

What is the difference between HR consulting and HR outsourcing?

HR consulting focuses on high-level strategic advisory, framework architecture, policy design, and process optimization. HR outsourcing involves executing daily administrative operations such as onboarding coordination and employee records maintenance.

Can you provide HRMS software selection consulting?

Yes. We act as an independent technology advisor. We define software requirements, author RFP evaluation rubrics, objectively compare commercial HRMS vendors, and oversee system adoption.

Do you provide POSH awareness consulting in India?

Yes. We provide policy drafting advisory, employee awareness training frameworks, and procedural guidance for Internal Committees (IC) under the Indian POSH Act, 2013.

How are consulting fees calculated?

Fees are structured around engagement scope, organization headcount, project duration, and agreed deliverable milestones. We provide detailed, transparent proposals following an initial discovery meeting.

Can HR consulting services be customized to our needs?

Absolutely. Every engagement is modular. Organizations can commission targeted interventions, such as a standalone policy manual or performance framework, or comprehensive enterprise transformations.

How does an HR consulting engagement begin?

Engagements typically begin with an introductory discovery consultation to evaluate your organizational scale, people management challenges, and commercial milestones, followed by a formal scope proposal.
